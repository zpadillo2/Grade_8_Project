Biology is the scientific study of life and living organisms, encompassing their structure, function, growth, evolution, and distribution. It investigates life across scales, from molecular interactions and cellular mechanisms to entire ecosystems. Key principles include cell theory, genetics, 
energy processing, homeostasis, and evolution by natural selection. 
Core Concepts: Biology relies on foundational concepts 
like the cell as the basic unit of life, DNA as the basis of heredity, 
and homeostasis for maintaining internal stability.
Fundamental Themes: Living things, or organisms, are 
characterized by their ability to consume energy, respond to stimuli, 
reproduce, and adapt to their environments.
