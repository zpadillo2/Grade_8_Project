Earth science, or geoscience, is the comprehensive study of 
planet Earth and its neighboring celestial bodies in the solar system. 
It is a multidisciplinary field that integrates physical, chemical, and biological 
processes to understand the planet's four major interacting spheres: the geosphere 
(solid earth), hydrosphere (water), atmosphere (air), and biosphere (living things). 
By studying Earth's materials, history, and active processes, scientists work to interpret 
the planet’s evolution, predict natural hazards like earthquakes and volcanic eruptions, 
and locate vital natural resources. The field is commonly divided into four main branches: 
geology (study of solid earth), meteorology (study of the atmosphere and weather), 
oceanography (study of ocean processes), and astronomy (study of Earth’s place in space).
